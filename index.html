Custom Printed, Certified Paper Food Packaging for Restaurants, Cafes and Cloud Kitchens

Bell EcoPacs, a division of The Bell Match Company, is a food packaging manufacturer in Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu. We make kraft food containers, takeaway and burger boxes, fold boxes, paper bags and paper straws for restaurant chains, cafes, bakeries, cloud kitchens, hotels and distributors. Our printing and converting site is BRCGS Grade A certified for direct food contact, and our quality system is ISO 9001:2015 certified. Every order is printed, die-cut and folded in our own factory, so you get consistent quality and dependable wholesale food packaging supply across India.
