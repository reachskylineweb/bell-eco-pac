Are you a food packaging manufacturer or a trader?
We manufacture. Printing, die-cutting, folding and gluing happen in our own factory in Virudhunagar district, Tamil Nadu.
Is your food packaging food safe?
Our production site is BRCGS Grade A certified for direct food contact printed paper boxes and paper straws. The certificate number (IN26/00000313) and site code (10022903) are published on this page, so you can check them on the BRCGS Directory.
Are you ISO certified?
Yes. Our quality management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015 by DNV (Certificate no. 176509-2015-AQ-IND-RvA), and has been continuously certified since November 2009.
Do you offer customised food packaging?
Yes. We print your logo and brand colours, match Pantone references and make custom sizes and die lines.
Can I place bulk wholesale orders?
Yes. We supply wholesale food packaging to restaurants, cloud kitchens, bakeries, hotels and distributors across India.
Is paper food packaging better than plastic?
Paper is made from a renewable resource and can be recycled where facilities exist. It also gives brands a premium look while cutting plastic use. Suitability depends on the food, so tell us what you pack and we will advise.
Do you make compostable packaging?
Our standard range is paper and kraft board. Compostable packaging claims need a test report for the exact product, so ask our team which items are covered.
Can I visit the factory?
Yes, commercial buyers are welcome to visit our plant in Virudhunagar district by appointment. Call +91 78459 65010 or email dm@bellmatch.com to book a tour.
